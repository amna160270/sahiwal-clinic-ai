Fever is a rise in body temperature above 38°C.

Common symptoms of fever:
- high temperature
- chills
- sweating
- headache
- weakness

Causes:
- viral infection
- bacterial infection

Headache symptoms:
- pain in head
- pressure
- sensitivity to light

Cough symptoms:
- throat irritation
- dry or wet cough
- chest discomfort

Always consult a doctor if symptoms last more than 3 days.
